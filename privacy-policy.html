Privacy policy.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

01Introduction

Aitie ("we", "us", "our") operates the Aitie platform and mobile application (the "Service"). We are a Data Fiduciary as defined under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA"), and this policy sets out how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal data belonging to you, the Data Principal, when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of your personal data as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

02Definitions

TermMeaning
Personal DataAny data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
Data PrincipalThe individual to whom the personal data relates — that's you. Where the individual is a child, includes their parent or lawful guardian.
Data FiduciaryThe entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data — that's us (Aitie).
ProcessingAny operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, sharing and erasure.
Consent ManagerA registered platform through which you may give, manage, review or withdraw consent, where applicable.

03Personal data we collect

We collect only the personal data that is necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Data you provide directly

  • Identity and contact details, such as name, email address, phone number
  • Account information, such as username, profile details, and work history
  • Payment or billing details, processed via our secure payment partners
  • Any information you submit through forms, support requests, or communications with us

Data collected automatically

  • Device and log information, such as IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage data, such as pages visited, features used, and time spent on the Service
  • Cookies and similar essential tracking technologies

04How we use your data

We process your personal data only for the specified purpose for which you have given consent, or for a legitimate use recognised under the DPDPA. These purposes include:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining the Service
  • Creating and managing your account and WorkPassport
  • Communicating with you about updates, security alerts, and support
  • Sending you relevant updates and marketing communications, where you have opted in
  • Improving and personalising the Service
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, abuse, or technical issues
  • Complying with our legal obligations

05Consent

We collect and process your personal data based on your free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous consent, communicated through clear affirmative action. Each consent request is accompanied by a notice, in clear and plain language, describing the personal data being collected and the purpose of processing.

You may withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as it was given. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and where applicable, we will stop further processing within a reasonable time of your withdrawal. Where we use a registered Consent Manager, you may also manage your consent through that platform.

In some cases, we may process personal data without consent where permitted under the DPDPA — for example, for compliance with a law, in response to a medical emergency, or for purposes of employment, to the extent applicable.

06Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Data Processors who process personal data on our behalf and under our instructions, such as hosting, analytics, payment, and customer support providers
  • Legal and regulatory authorities, where required by law, court order, or governmental request
  • Business transfers, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent protection of your data

Any third party we share personal data with is bound by contract to process it only for the specified purpose and to apply reasonable security safeguards.

07Cross-border data transfer

Your personal data may be stored or processed outside India, including by service providers such as cloud hosting infrastructure. Where we transfer personal data outside India, we do so in accordance with the DPDPA, and will not transfer data to any country or territory that the Central Government has restricted by notification.

08Data retention and erasure

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required to comply with a legal obligation, whichever is longer.

Where you withdraw consent or where the specified purpose is no longer being served, and retention is not otherwise required by law, we will erase your personal data and require our Data Processors to do the same, unless preservation is necessary for a legal proceeding.

09Security safeguards

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction, including encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, and regular security reviews.

In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required under the DPDPA.

10Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities carried out on it
  • Correction and updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected
  • Grievance redressal in relation to any act or omission by us regarding your personal data
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity
  • Withdraw consent at any time, in relation to any purpose you had previously consented to

To exercise these rights, please write to our Grievance Officer using the contact details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and, in any case, in line with the timelines prescribed under the DPDPA and its rules.

11Children's data

Where our Service is directed at, or knowingly used by, children (individuals below 18 years of age), we obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing the child's personal data.

We do not undertake processing of children's personal data that is likely to cause detrimental effect on their wellbeing, and we do not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India, established under the DPDPA, which is empowered to inquire into complaints and impose penalties for non-compliance.

The Data Protection Board of India's contact details and complaint process are published on its official website once operationalised by the Government of India.

Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
