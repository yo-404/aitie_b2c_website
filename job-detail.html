Senior / Lead Flutter Developer

We are looking for an experienced Senior Flutter Developer (5+ Years Exp) to spearhead our cross-platform mobile ecosystem, build fluid 60fps experiences, and shape the mobile future of independent work for creators worldwide.

About the Role

At Aitie, our mobile application is the primary hub where independent consultants, agencies, and high-growth builders manage workflows, negotiate real-time agreements, and collaborate seamlessly.

As a Senior Flutter Developer, you will own our mobile architecture end-to-end. You will work closely with founders, product designers, and backend systems engineers to craft world-class iOS and Android experiences that feel instant, delightful, and unbreakable.

What You'll Do

  • Architect & Ship: Build and scale clean, maintainable cross-platform mobile apps for iOS and Android using Flutter & Dart.
  • Fluid Interfaces: Implement rich micro-interactions, responsive design systems, gesture-driven navigations, and custom animations that feel native on both platforms.
  • Real-Time & Offline Sync: Integrate WebSockets, background syncing, robust caching layers (Isar / Hive / SQLite), and REST/GraphQL APIs.
  • Native Bridging: Write native code (Swift / Kotlin) using MethodChannels to leverage device-specific APIs, sensors, push notifications, and background services.
  • Quality & CI/CD: Establish automated unit, widget, and integration testing frameworks; manage App Store and Play Store releases with automated pipelines.

Requirements (What We're Looking For)

  • 5+ Years of Experience: Proven background in professional software engineering with at least 3+ years specifically deploying production-scale Flutter applications.
  • Deep Dart & Flutter Mastery: In-depth understanding of widget trees, RenderObjects, custom painters, memory management, and frame profiling.
  • State Management: Expert hands-on experience with Bloc, Riverpod, or modern reactive patterns in Flutter.
  • Store Deployment Track Record: Have published, monitored, and scaled apps on Apple App Store & Google Play Store with thousands of active users.
  • Native Know-How: Comfortable writing Swift / Kotlin platform channel integrations when needed.

Tech Stack & Tools

Flutter 3.x Dart Bloc / Riverpod Swift & Kotlin REST / GraphQL WebSockets Isar / Hive Firebase Fastlane & CI/CD Git / GitHub
