Our Story

We started with a simple observation: the best talent was already choosing independence, on purpose. 53% of Gen Z already work independently. More importantly, 45% of high-growth companies now build independent talent into their core teams, and 65% of global leaders plan to open their doors even wider.

Even though India already had the largest independent workforce in the world, over 15 million strong, there was still no real system for them.

And this wasn't a small shift happening on the side. By mid-2025, 36.3% of new startups were solo-founded, entire companies now run almost completely on independent talent: design here, development there, legal somewhere else, no single team under one roof. We know, because that's exactly how Aitie itself came together.

And yet, every platform built for this world was built for the client first. The professional was always the one adjusting, the one proving, the one waiting to be chosen.

We wanted to build something different: an app built for the workforce first, where every feature exists to make their life easier, not just the client’s.

So that's what we set out to build with Aitie.

Companies noticed the shift too, and wanted a way in. That became Aitie Edge, built on the same trust, just from the other side of the table.
