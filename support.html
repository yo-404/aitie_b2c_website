03Common questions

Accounts and sign-in

I’m not receiving my OTP.
Check that the number or email is the one on your account, look in spam, and wait for the resend timer before trying again. SMS to Indian networks can occasionally be delayed by a few minutes. If nothing arrives after two attempts, email us and we will check delivery logs from our side.
I’ve forgotten my password.
Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. You can also sign in with a verified one-time code sent to your registered email or phone number.
Can I change my registered email or phone number?
Yes — navigate to Settings → Profile. You will need to verify the new email or number before the change takes effect.
Why was I signed out of all my devices?
Signing out anywhere, changing your password, or an administrative security lock takes effect immediately on every active device. If you did not initiate any of these, reset your password and contact support immediately.

Your Work Passport

My CV import got things wrong.
The import is AI-assisted and designed as a smart starting point. You can edit, reorder, or delete any entry directly. The uploaded source document itself is deleted automatically within 24 hours.
How do I share my passport publicly?
Turn on public sharing in your passport settings and copy your personalized link. You can switch it back to private, or regenerate the link (which immediately invalidates the old link) at any time.
How is my Aitie Score calculated?
The score is computed from verified records of your activity on the platform: profile completeness, proof of work, experience, and successful engagement outcomes. It is not a subjective average of user ratings. You can inspect your breakdown and recent trend in the app. Companies see only your tier label, never the exact numeric score.

Job Pools

My application is "pending review" — how long does that take?
Applications are carefully reviewed by human specialists, typically within 2–3 business days. You will receive an in-app notification and email either way.
I was rejected. Can I apply again?
Usually yes, after a cooldown period, subject to attempt limits. The app displays the exact date when you become eligible to re-apply.
Can I retake an assessment?
Re-sits are allowed subject to the pool cooldown window. Your previous attempt is archived for auditing and never erased.
I’m in a pool but not receiving invitations.
Invitations depend on active hiring needs, skill match relevance, and ranking within the pool. Pool membership certifies eligibility but does not guarantee direct work assignment.

Work and payment

When do I get paid?
When the client company approves your milestone, month, or consultation session. The funds, less the applicable platform service fee, are released to your wallet immediately upon approval.
Approval is final — what does that mean?
Once a company approves a milestone, the fund release completes irrevocably through the platform. If something is incomplete or requires revision, raise it with the other party before authorizing approval.
Why can’t I withdraw funds?
Common reasons include pending identity verification (KYC), incomplete payment payout setup, or funds subject to an unresolved dispute. The app specifies the exact requirement.
Where are funds held?
Funds are held in compliance with RBI escrow regulations by our licensed banking and payment partner Razorpay — not in a general company bank account. Your Aitie wallet reflects funds safeguarded for you until withdrawal.

Identity verification (KYC)

Why do you need my PAN and Aadhaar?
Indian financial and tax regulations mandate identity verification before payouts can be issued. These details are stored in an encrypted, isolated repository and are never disclosed to clients or other professionals.
My verification was rejected.
The exact rejection cause is highlighted in the app — typically an unreadable photo, glare, or a name discrepancy with official government records. Simply correct and resubmit.

Privacy and your data

How do I get a copy of my data?
Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Download my data. Your portable archive is generated automatically, available up to five times per day.
How do I delete my account?
Visit our Delete Your Data guide. Account deletion includes a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel simply by logging back in.

04Reporting a problem with another user

If someone on Aitie has behaved abusively, misrepresented their qualifications, or attempted to take an engagement off the platform, please report it immediately. Tap Report in the app on the relevant profile or conversation, or email safety@aitie.app.

Include full context, timestamps, and screenshots. Reports are reviewed by our Trust & Safety team within 24 hours. We never disclose the identity of the person filing the report.

Never accept payments or work outside the platform. Moving engagements off-platform forfeits your escrow guarantee, milestone tracking, and dispute protection, and directly breaches our non-circumvention policies.

05Reporting a security vulnerability

If you have identified a vulnerability or potential security concern, please inform us confidentially at security@aitie.app before making any public disclosure. Include clear steps to reproduce the issue.

We commit to acknowledging your report within 2 business days and keeping you updated on remediation progress. Aitie will not pursue legal action against researchers acting in good faith without accessing or altering customer data.
